# 发布时间与传播窗口

## Core Principle

Match publication time to the reader's emotional window. For 阿Der content, the best time is when readers are already thinking about work, money, fatigue, AI anxiety, or the next workday.

Do not treat time as the only variable. Track title, topic, cover, reposting, and comments together.

## Default Time Slots

Use these as starting points:

- Monday 21:00-22:00: best for work anxiety, Sunday-night/weekday pressure spillover, AI workload, "tomorrow still has work" themes.
- Wednesday 12:00-12:30: best for light workplace jokes, lunch-break reading, simple four-panel comics.
- Thursday 21:30-22:30: best for emotional resonance, adult self-mockery, money/fatigue topics.
- Sunday 20:30-22:00: best for "tomorrow is Monday", workplace pre-anxiety, resignation impulse, low-energy adult themes.

Use second-best slots:
- Workday 07:30-08:45 for commute-friendly short posts.
- Workday 18:00-19:30 for after-work resentment and overtime topics.
- Workday 22:30-23:30 for sharper late-night emotional posts.

Avoid by default:
- Saturday morning before 10:30 for workplace topics.
- After 00:00 unless deliberately testing late-night readers.
- Workday 15:00-17:00 for new posts unless reposting to groups.

## Topic-to-Time Matching

AI/tool/workload topics:
- Best: Monday 21:30, Thursday 22:00
- Reason: readers are thinking about efficiency, work pressure, and "why tools did not make life easier".

Overtime/downstream task topics:
- Best: Workday 18:30-19:30 or 21:30
- Reason: readers are leaving work or failing to leave work.

Money/salary/expense topics:
- Best: Payday evening if known; otherwise Thursday 21:30 or Sunday 21:00
- Reason: private anxiety works better at night.

Meeting/boss phrase/office absurdity topics:
- Best: Wednesday 12:10 or Monday 21:30
- Reason: lunch break supports light吐槽; Monday night supports accumulated work resentment.

Sunday/Monday anxiety topics:
- Best: Sunday 20:30-22:00
- Reason: mood is already primed.

Weekend posts:
- Use weekend only for life, money, relationships, AI learning, or "成年人低功耗" topics.
- Avoid Saturday morning for pure workplace posts.

## Repost Plan

If initial read is low:
1. Do not delete immediately.
2. Repost to朋友圈/社群 at 21:00-22:00 with a sharper hook.
3. Ask one concrete comment question.
4. Track whether reads move after repost.

Example repost copy:

公司里有些问题，不是没人知道。
是大家都知道，但没人负责看见。

你们公司有没有这种长期存在、但没人处理的问题？

## Output Requirement

When making a complete issue package, include:
- Recommended publish day and time
- Backup publish time
- Why this time matches the topic
- Repost window
- Repost copy
- Data to track after publishing
